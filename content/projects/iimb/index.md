- Advised by Prof. Srinivasan Rangan and PhD student Prabhu Venkatachalam.
- Implemented a pipeline to extract textual and numeric data from public financial filings of firms for an academic project developing a new measure of Disclosure Quality.
- Extracted data from ~60,000 firms.
- Developed all stages of the data science pipeline, from web scraping to cleaning to analysis and visualization.
- Developed textual analysis methods at a low level using regular expressions, and at a contextual level using regex and SciPy.
