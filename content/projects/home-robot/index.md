Research project with Jiaming Hu, [Anwesan Pal](https://anwesanpal.github.io/), Akanimoh Adeleye, Aditya Aggarwal, and Prof. Henrik Christensen at CogRob, UCSD.

- Developing an assistant robot that can help humans with everyday household cleanup tasks.
- Worked on task planning and system architecture integration to orchestrate the robot's behavior.
- Presented at the [IEEE Robotic Computing Conference 2023](https://www.ieee-irc.org/). [Paper link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.06129.pdf).
- Project [website](https://sites.google.com/eng.ucsd.edu/home-robot).
