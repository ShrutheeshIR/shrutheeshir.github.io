Our most recent work, "Motion Planning in Foliated Manifolds using Repetition Roadmap," has been accepted at [Robotics: Science and Systems (RSS) 2024](https://www.roboticsproceedings.org/rss20/), held in Delft, Netherlands. It was presented on July 16, 2024. The conference paper can be found [here](https://www.roboticsproceedings.org/rss20/p036.pdf).
