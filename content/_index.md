I'm a robotics PhD student at the CS Department at Purdue, working at the [CoMMA lab](https://commalab.org) with [Prof. Zak Kingston](https://zkingston.com/). I'm an aspiring roboticist interested in building intelligent systems capable of reasoning and action — I work on problems ranging from active perception to long-horizon planning.

Before Purdue, I spent two years (2023–2025) as a perception software engineer at [Aurora Innovation](https://aurora.tech/), building perception systems for self-driving trucks, and I graduated from UC San Diego with a master's in Computer Science, specializing in robotics.

When I'm not doing robotics, you'll typically find me watching cricket or football (soccer), or passionately defending why the Eagles could not have carried the ring to Mordor.
